FAQ

惜宝（Shepaw）到底是什么？What exactly is Shepaw?

你的 Agent 总管。你说一句话，它帮你把活派给合适的 Agent、串起流程、盯到出结果。底层是一套给智能体设计的即时通讯：Agent 是有身份、有名片、有信箱的联系人，而不是聊天软件里的机器人。

The steward for your agents. Say it once and it hands the job to a suitable agent, strings the steps into a flow, and stays on it until there is a result. Underneath is an instant messenger built for agents: they are contacts with identity, profiles, and inboxes, not chatbots bolted onto a messenger.

和 ChatGPT / 微信里加个 AI 有什么不同？How is this different from ChatGPT or AI inside WeChat?

它们是云端的通用助手，擅长聊天，却碰不到你机器上的东西。惜宝不跟它们比聊天，它是你这台主机的总管：调度你本机的 Agent，产物落进你自己的储物袋，开源、自托管、端到端加密，不依赖任何一家大厂。

Those are general-purpose cloud assistants: good at chatting, but unable to touch what is on your machine. Shepaw does not compete on chat. It is the steward of your host: it directs the agents on your machine, artifacts land in your own pouch, and it is open source, self-hostable, end-to-end encrypted, and independent of any vendor.

储物袋是什么？What is the pouch?

主机上的内容寻址共享空间，用 pouch:// URI 存文件、快照、记忆和工作区。手机和其他客户端连上这台主机后直接读写同一份产物；数据留在主机本地，不需要云服务。

A content-addressed shared space on the host, addressed by pouch:// URIs for files, snapshots, memory, and workspaces. The phone and other clients read and write the same artifacts once connected to that host; data stays local, with no cloud service needed.

这是免费的吗？Is this free?

是。Shepaw、shepaw-cli 与 channel 都公开源码。你需要自备 Claude Code、Gemini、Qwen Code、Copilot 等引擎的账号与凭据。Channel 可自托管，也可用官方实例。

Yes. Shepaw, shepaw-cli, and channel publish their source. Bring your own Claude Code, Gemini, Qwen Code, Copilot, or other engine credentials. Channel is self-hostable; a hosted instance is optional.

整套都能自己部署吗？Can I self-host the whole stack?

可以。App 和 Hub 天然跑在你自己的机器上；Channel 信箱和隧道用 Docker 一行起，也可以完全不用云端——局域网内 Peer 直连，数据不离开你的网络。

Yes. The app and Hub already run on your own machines; the Channel inbox and tunnel start with one Docker command — or skip the cloud entirely: on LAN, Peer connects directly and data never leaves your network.

我的代码会离开这台机器吗？Does my code leave my machine?

Agent 在你自己的电脑或服务器上跑，像你平时开 CLI 一样访问仓库。Channel 只转发 Noise 密文和收件箱密文，看不到聊天内容、方法名或密钥。局域网 Peer 可以完全不经过 Channel。

Agents run on your computer or server and touch the repo the way the CLI already does. Channel relays Noise ciphertext and sealed inbox blobs — no chat text, method names, or keys. On LAN you can skip Channel entirely.

必须装桌面 App 吗？Do I need the desktop app?

不必。机器上装 shepaw-cli（命令是 shepaw）即可，用手机 App 扫码。桌面 App 用来在电脑上聊、管本地 LLM 和群组。

No. Install shepaw-cli (the command is shepaw) on the machine, then pair from the phone app. The desktop app is for chatting on the computer, local LLMs, and groups.

如何从外网连回家？Can I connect from outside my network?

在 Hub 里配置 Channel（官方 channel.shepaw.com 或自托管），重新生成带 channel= 的配对码。也可以用 Tailscale 等隧道走 Peer 直连。

Configure Channel in the hub (hosted channel.shepaw.com or self-hosted) and re-pair so the QR includes channel=. Or expose Peer over Tailscale and skip Channel.

会不会被模型厂商封号？Can I get banned for using Shepaw?

我们不能代替厂商承诺。Shepaw 把各家本地 CLI 当子进程拉起，不抽 token、不直连推理 API。对厂商来说，这和你自己在终端里跑没有区别。

We cannot promise on a provider’s behalf. Shepaw launches each vendor CLI as a subprocess. It does not extract tokens or call inference APIs directly — usage looks like you ran the CLI yourself.
